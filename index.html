How spacing works

Didn't know brings a card back in 3–5 cards, Partially in 10–15, Nailed it tomorrow, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Cards you keep missing come back sooner. A card counts as mastered after you nail it on 3 separate days. Progress is saved on this device.
